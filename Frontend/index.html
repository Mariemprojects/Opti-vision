Frequently Asked Questions

Need Help? We've Got You Covered!

What is Opti-Vision?

Opti-Vision is an AI-powered computer vision service that provides image classification, object detection, and document analysis capabilities. Our platform helps businesses automate visual content analysis and extract valuable insights from images and documents.

How accurate are your AI models?

Our AI models are trained on diverse datasets and continuously improved to ensure high accuracy. However, the accuracy may vary depending on the specific use case and data quality. We recommend conducting a pilot test to evaluate performance for your particular application.

What types of images can I classify?

You can classify various image types including photographs, product images, medical imagery, and more. Our system supports common formats like JPG, PNG, and BMP, with a maximum file size of 10MB per image.

How many objects can be detected in a single image?

Our system can detect multiple objects simultaneously in a single image, with no theoretical limit. However, performance may vary with very crowded scenes or overlapping objects.

How accurate is the handwritten text recognition?

Our AI-powered OCR achieves high accuracy for handwritten text, though results depend on handwriting clarity. We recommend using high-quality scans (300+ DPI) for best results with handwritten content.
